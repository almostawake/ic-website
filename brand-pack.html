02 — Brand Concept

Built for the Overlooked Rider

The equestrian industry caters to children and elite competitors. Amateur horse owners are assumed to be elite — or invisible. Inner Circle exists for them.

Everything Under One Roof

Agistment, lessons, horse schooling, and competition support in one place. No juggling multiple providers, no fragmented care. Your horse and your progress, managed together with consistency and trust.

Agistment + Training + Events

Your Pace, No Pressure

We assume no previous knowledge. Whether you're rebuilding confidence after a fall, learning from scratch, or just want to enjoy your horse without the pressure to compete — this is your space.

Patience + Empathy + Safety

A Community That Gets It

Everyone here is in the same boat — amateur horse owners who love their horses and want to learn without feeling out of place. Every ribbon, every breakthrough, every small win gets celebrated together.

Belonging + Milestones + Fun

05 — Typography

Type System

Three fonts. Display warmth from Cormorant Garamond, structural clarity from Montserrat, technical precision from JetBrains Mono.

Cormorant Garamond

Display & Editorial

High-contrast serif with an elegant, equestrian heritage feel. Used for headlines, taglines, and pull quotes. The italic weight carries the brand's warmth — matching the "Supported by Natalie Innes" lockup in the logo.

Meet the Team Our Services

Agistment

Individual private paddocks, horse-safe fencing, twice-daily feeding and checking. A lit indoor arena, outdoor jumping, wash bays, trails, and a genuine community to share it with.

Lessons & Schooling

Bespoke training that meets you where you are. Ground work, flatwork, jumping, dressage — at your pace. Nat schools your horse too, so progress never stalls.

Events & Community

Competitions, clinics, beach rides, trail rides, social events. We load up, travel together, and support each other on the day. You never have to do it alone.
