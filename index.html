Meet Natalie

Fun, inclusive, and endlessly patient

Natalie Innes has spent 37 years with horses at every level from grassroots to international. A British Horse Society Accredited Coach and Equestrian Australia qualified instructor, she's built her career around one belief: every rider deserves to feel supported.

After running a successful equestrian business in the UK, Nat brought her experience to the Southern Highlands — creating a space where adult amateurs can learn, grow, and enjoy their horses without pressure or judgement.

"I've been where many of my riders are — nervous, unsure, wanting to do right by my horse. That's exactly why I do this."

What We Offer

Everything under one roof

Agistment

Calm, community-focused agistment with individual private paddocks, horse-safe fencing, twice daily feeding, checking and rugging. Your horse is in safe hands — so you can relax.

Lessons

Bespoke training and lessons tailored to you — whether you're rebuilding confidence, working on groundwork, or preparing to get back in the saddle. No rush, no assumptions.

Horse Training & Schooling

Professional schooling aligned with your goals and your horse's temperament. We respect the bond between you and your horse — training is always done with care and in consultation with you.

Event Support

When you're ready, we'll help you load up, travel to events, and support you on the day — so you never have to do it alone. From your first outing to your personal best, we're right there with you.

Plus: clinics with external professionals, trail rides, beach rides, and social events.

The Property

Calgary Park, Berrima

Set in the heart of the Southern Highlands, Calgary Park offers a peaceful, purpose-built equestrian property surrounded by beautiful Australian bushland — perfect for exploring and enjoying.

  • ✓ Fully lit indoor arena — 65m x 35m, Soiltex surface
  • ✓ Outdoor show jumping area
  • ✓ Private trail riding through native bushland
  • ✓ 5 undercover holding yards adjacent to arena
