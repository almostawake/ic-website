The Gap

The equestrian world wasn't built for you.

The industry caters to children and elite competitors. If you're an amateur horse owner — someone who rides for the love of it, fits horses around work and life, and just wants to do right by their horse — you're often left to figure it out alone.

Feeling stuck, nervous, or doing it alone? You don't have to be.

Inner Circle Equestrian exists for riders like you — a place where you and your horse get the support you both deserve, without pressure, without judgement, and without assumptions about where you "should" be.

What We Offer

Everything under one roof

From daily care to competition day — we're with you at every step.

Agistment

A safe, supported home for your horse

Calm, community-focused agistment with individual private paddocks, horse-safe fencing, twice daily feeding, checking and rugging. Your horse is in safe hands — so you can relax.

Lessons

Tailored to you, not a template

Bespoke training and lessons — whether you're rebuilding confidence, working on groundwork, or preparing to get back in the saddle. No rush, no assumptions.

Horse Training

Professional schooling, your way

Professional schooling aligned with your goals and your horse's temperament. Training is always done with care and in consultation with you — we respect the bond between you and your horse.

Event Support

You never have to do it alone

When you're ready, we'll help you load up, travel to events, and support you on the day. From your first outing to your personal best, we're right there with you.

Plus: clinics with external professionals, trail rides, beach rides, and social events.

Meet Natalie

Fun, inclusive, and endlessly patient

Natalie Innes has spent 37 years with horses at every level — from grassroots to international. A British Horse Society Accredited Coach and Equestrian Australia qualified instructor, she's built her career around one belief: every rider deserves to feel supported.

After running a successful equestrian business in the UK, Nat brought her experience to the Southern Highlands — creating a space where amateur horse owners can learn, grow, and enjoy their horses without pressure or judgement.

"I've been where many of my riders are — nervous, unsure, wanting to do right by my horse. That's exactly why I do this."

The Property

Calgary Park, Berrima

Set in the heart of the Southern Highlands, Calgary Park is a peaceful, purpose-built equestrian property surrounded by beautiful Australian bushland — perfect for exploring and enjoying.

  • ✓ Fully lit indoor arena — 65m × 35m, Soiltex surface
  • ✓ Outdoor show jumping area
  • ✓ Private trail riding through native bushland
  • ✓ 5 undercover holding yards adjacent to arena
  • ✓ 4 wash bays including hot water
  • ✓ Stables, tack room, and day yards
  • ✓ Tea, coffee, bathroom, and shower facilities
